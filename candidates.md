# Candidates — The Moon Finally Answers Back (gate: 1–240 chars)

Chosen (108 chars):
> Four billion years of wishes, howls and 2 a.m. promises, and not one of you has ever wished me a good night.

Why it wins: answers all three brief elements (wishes, howls, late-night
promises) in one breath, in the Moon's own wry, old, tired voice; the turn
lands in the final clause; one read, no explanation attached.

   Good Moon voice, but lists jobs instead of answering; weaker comeback turn.

   Wry and tired, but answers staring, not the wishes/howls/promises.

   Punchy, but the Tuesday payoff is arbitrary and needs a beat to land.

   Covers all three, but the turn is soft and the line spends characters twice saying the same thing.
